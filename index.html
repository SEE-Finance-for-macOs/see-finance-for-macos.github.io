SEE Finance

Comprehensive personal finance manager for Mac. Track banking, credit cards, investments, and more in one secure place. Generate customizable reports, monitor budgets, and handle 150+ currencies with full control over your data.

Unified Account Management

Centralize all financial accounts—banking, credit cards, investments—into a single, intuitive interface. Import OFX, QFX, CSV, QIF, and QMTF files effortlessly. Export to CSV, TXF, or QIF for seamless integration.

Investment Tracking

Monitor individual investments and your entire portfolio with precision. Track performance, balances, and gains in real-time across multiple currencies. Split transactions and attach supporting documents for complete oversight.

Advanced Reporting and Budgeting

Create in-depth, customizable reports to visualize spending patterns. Set budgets, track adherence, and schedule recurring transactions. Switch between calendar and list views for optimal workflow.

Personalization and Security

Tailor the app with color coding, adjustable fonts, batch editing, and quick entry options. Store files on iCloud Drive for secure access across Mac, iPhone, and iPad—your data stays under your control, never uploaded to external services.
